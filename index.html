A little journey through their story

Chapter One

When We Met

2.5 Years Ago…

Kabhi-kabhi zindagi ek aise insaan se milwati hai, jiske baare mein humne kabhi socha bhi nahi hota. Palak aur Kishan ki kahaani bhi kuch aisi hi shuru hui. Ek mulaakaat, kuch baatein, aur dheere-dheere ek aisa rishta jo waqt ke saath aur khaas hota gaya. Shayad us waqt unhe bhi nahi pata tha ki yeh chhoti si shuruaat ek din unki zindagi ka itna khoobsurat hissa banegi.

Chapter Two

We Became More Than Friends

Phir waqt guzarta gaya… baatein badhti gayi, aur ek-doosre ki fikr bhi. Jo pehle sirf ek mulaakaat thi, woh dheere-dheere ek gehri understanding ban gayi. Ek-doosre ki khushi mein khush hona, chhoti-chhoti baaton ko yaad rakhna, aur har din ek nayi yaad banana — unka rishta ab pehle se kahin zyada khaas ho chuka tha. Kuch rishte naam se nahi, ehsaas se pehchaane jaate hain.

Chapter Three

A New Beginning

You brought a marriage proposal home

Aur phir aaya woh pal, jab unki kahaani ne ek naya mod liya. Kishan ke parivaar ki taraf se Palak ke ghar shaadi ka rishta aaya. Ghar mein baatein hui, parivaar ek-doosre ko samajhne lage, aur is khoobsurat rishte ko apnane ka faisla hua. Ek nayi umeed, ek naya sapna, aur do parivaaron ke beech judta hua ek pyaara sa rishta. Ab unki kahaani sirf do logon ki nahi, do parivaaron ki bhi ban rahi thi.
